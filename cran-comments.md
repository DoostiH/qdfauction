## Comments

The package implements estimators from Doosti, Dewan and Talebian (2025,
Economics Letters, <doi:10.1016/j.econlet.2025.112670>) and Doosti (2026,
Economics Letters, <doi:10.1016/j.econlet.2026.112837>); two further papers are
cited as working papers.

Parts of the package are R translations of code by other authors
(Chaubey, Dewan and Li; Hickman and Hubbard; Vidakovic; Marmer and
Shneyerov; Zincenko). They are listed as contributors and copyright
holders in Authors@R, the origin of each file is documented in
inst/COPYRIGHTS, and the authors have been informed.
